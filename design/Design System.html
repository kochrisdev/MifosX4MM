Principles

How the system thinks
01
Warm, calm, deliberate

Warm off-white surface, navy text, teal accents. Amber reserved exclusively for risk and overdue states — no decorative use.

Hairlines, not shadows

Cards and surfaces are separated by 1px borders (--border) — never drop shadows or heavy elevation.

Tabular numerics

All financial figures use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums so columns of numbers align.

Clear primary action

One teal-filled primary CTA per screen, anchored in the page header. Everything else is outline or ghost.

Colors

Tokenized palette — reference by CSS variable, not by hex
